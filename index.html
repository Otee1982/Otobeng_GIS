Selected GIS & Analytics Work

Four spatial problems, four geographies, one analytical approach.

39.29°N, 76.61°W · BALTIMORE, MD

Food Deserts & Diabetes in Baltimore

Problem

Where do food deserts and elevated diabetes prevalence overlap across the city's neighborhoods?

Method

Multi-layer vector overlay — city boundaries, neighborhood polygons, dialysis-center points — symbolized as a prevalence choropleth.

Result

Surfaced neighborhoods facing compounded food insecurity and diabetes burden, informing targeted outreach.

QGISVector OverlayChoroplethPublic Health
44.47°N, 72.69°W · STOWE, VT

Stowe Ski Resort — Terrain Suitability

Problem

Which parcels are geomorphologically suited to new ski-run development?

Method

Slope and contour layers derived from high-resolution DEMs; multi-criteria evaluation against elevation and gradient thresholds.

Result

Flagged candidate sites that balance terrain advantage with minimal environmental disturbance.

QGISDEM AnalysisSlope ModelingMulti-Criteria Eval
21.12°S, 55.54°E · RÉUNION ISLAND

Demographic Cartography of Réunion (2019)

Problem

How is the island's population distributed across municipalities, and where are the density extremes?

Method

Cartographic dashboard pairing a municipal population choropleth with EPCI bar charts and density pie charts.

Result

A one-glance demographic read of urban cores versus rural areas to support regional planning.

REGIONAL EXTENT · WESTERN U.S.

Sage-Grouse Habitat & Federal Land Ownership

Problem

Where does Greater Sage-grouse habitat intersect federal land jurisdictions across the West?

Method

Large-scale overlay of habitat polygons against BLM, Forest Service, NPS and other federal ownership layers.

Result

Clarified the multi-agency footprint behind habitat protection, supporting coordinated conservation policy.
